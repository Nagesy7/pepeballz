# Ribbit Nugs: Cate's Fury

An Inu pack adventure: every villager is an Inu, the enemies are Cate the Catnip Queen (an upright grey tabby with a crown and cape) and her ginger tabby Catnip Kits, and cosmetics tie into $KIBBLE.

Display title comes from `src/config.js` (`GAME_TITLE`). The repo, URL (pepeballz.vercel.app), save keys and kibble-pay game id stay `pepeballz`.

This is an original top-down action RPG built with vanilla JS and canvas. It has no frameworks and no build step.
- **Controls:** WASD/arrows to move, J/Z to attack (3-hit combo) or talk, K/X for Howl Wave, hold L/C to rest (recover energy), Space/Shift to dash, Q/V to Unleash, Enter for the menu, M or the speaker button to mute, E/B to use your hero power, R to swap powers. Touch devices get a thumb stick on the left and buttons on the right.
- **Controllers:** Bluetooth/USB PS5 DualSense, Xbox and generic pads are supported through the Gamepad API standard mapping (`src/gamepad.js`).
  - Moving: left stick (0.2 deadzone, adjustable in Sound & feel) or D-pad.
  - Fighting: A/Cross attacks or confirms, B/Circle dodges in play (back in menus), Y/Triangle is Howl Wave, X/Square uses the equipped power and R1/RB swaps it.
  - Other buttons: L1/LB rests, R2/RT unleashes, L2/LT also dodges, Create/View mutes, and Options/Menu pauses or closes menus.
  - Glyphs and prompts: a toast with PS, Xbox or generic glyphs (detected from `gamepad.id`: 054c or Sony is PS, 045e or Xbox is Xbox) appears on connect and disconnect. Button prompts show while the pad is in use.
  - Hot-plug and performance: pads are picked up through connect and disconnect events plus polling. Polling runs only while a pad is connected.
  - Fairness: the pad gives the same actions, speed and cooldowns as the keyboard, with no turbo or aim assist.
- **Ambient life:** dragonflies and fish ripples by the water, butterflies by day, fireflies at dusk (Sydney time, or `?time=night` / `?time=day`), birds, swaying grass and reeds, drifting leaves and villagers who idle and emote. Each area has its own WebAudio soundscape that only starts after your first tap or key press. Effects scale down on small screens or low FPS and pause while the tab is hidden. Menu > Sound & feel has the sound, volume, ambience, screen shake, hit-stop and effects settings, all saved locally.
- **Daily challenge:** one rotating challenge per Sydney calendar day, the same for everyone (`src/daily.js`). It is shown on the title screen and in the HUD. Rewards are free Bones plus a cosmetic. There are no wagers, fees or $KIBBLE costs, and it has no effect on prize boards. Progress is saved locally and works with no wallet.
- **Recent runs feed:** real recent scores from the kibble-pay prize boards (`/api/game?action=scores&game=pepeballz`, daily then weekly), with wallets shortened. When the boards are empty it says "No recent runs yet". Nothing is ever faked.
- **Hero powers (story mode):** Tail Spin (Lv 3), Pounce Dash (defeat 12 Catnip Kits) and Bark Shockwave (find 3 Pond Lilies). Each has a cooldown, can also be learned with free in-game Bones and is never sold for $KIBBLE. Power-ups drop in the field: Zoomies (speed), Bone Shield (blocks 3 hits), Kibble Magnet (pulls pickups, +50% Bones) and Lily Bloom (heal + regen), each with a HUD timer. Powers and power-ups are switched off in prize runs (Hunt / Boss Rush / Tourney), so the prize boards stay skill-only.
- **Move tiers + power-up upgrades (Menu > Powers, story mode, `src/powers.js`):** Bones earned in play only, never $KIBBLE, gated by player level.
  - Tail Spin T2 (Lv5, 60 Bones: x1.25 dmg, +5 reach, 2.6s) / T3 (Lv10, 140: x1.55, +10, 2.2s, swats enemy shots). Pounce Dash T2 (Lv5, 80: x1.25, +15 leap, 4.0s) / T3 (Lv10, 160: x1.55, +25 leap, 3.5s, landing dazes 0.6s). Bark Shockwave T2 (Lv6, 100: x1.2, +10 rings, stun 1.5s, 7s) / T3 (Lv12, 200: x1.45, +20, stun 1.8s, 6s, heals 5%).
  - Power-up upgrades L1-L3 (Lv4/8/12, 30/60/100 Bones) add duration or strength to Zoomies, Bone Shield, Kibble Magnet, Lily Bloom and the new Wildberry Snack (+30% attack for 8s).
  - Combos: Paw Twister (press power again within 0.7s of a Pounce landing for a x1.6 Tail Spin finisher), Stun Pounce (x1.5 on a foe dazed by Bark).
  - Collar look (cosmetic only) follows total tiers + upgrades: Plain Pup, Bronze Collar (3), Silver Collar (7), Gold Collar (12), Moonstone Coat (18). Each look recolours the coat, paints a collar band and leaves a coloured paw-print trail; stepping up plays a one-shot paw-burst. Moves get bigger, more colourful swirls and rings per tier. Prize runs show the look only, with zero stat effect (`tests/tiers.test.mjs`, `tests/e2e-tiers.mjs`).
- **Chapter 1:** Pawpaw Village, Whisker Woods and Fang Peak. The village pond has run dry: collect 5 Pond Lilies (Rose, Sun, Sky, Moss, Dusk), each a permanent buff, beat Cate (she drops the Dusk Lily) and set them in the basin to restore the pond. Pondo the Pond Toad moves back in. No wishes, no summoning.
- **Inu sprites:** ported from Inu Verse (`src/inu/` is a copy, so Inu Verse itself is untouched).
- **Pond Network (`src/kibble.js`):** real kibble-pay wallet by default (Phantom sign-in via `https://kibble-pay.vercel.app/inu-games.js`). $KIBBLE is live (CA `4VE9sy4fEMJdZ6i7MkYPbnqDJd2oqzJQmg44WJ3opump`): Wish Shop / Season purchases and opt-in Legend burns are real and signed in Phantom. Prices are fee-inclusive catalog prices (`pepeballz:<id>`). Owned cosmetics come from `/api/inventory`. Boss Rush / Hunt clears post a wallet-signed score (`/api/game`), and the leaderboards show the real kibble-pay boards and pools. Your Inu Verse Shiba shows in the Pond Network menu. Simulated rivals, payouts, bounties, raids and launches run **only in demo mode (`?demo=1`)**, which uses a separate save. The footer links Terms / Prize Rules. Legacy demo description: It includes a weekly seeded lily hunt, a Cate boss rush, rank-based daily/weekly/monthly pools, holder tiers, the Pond Shop (2% fee in millionths), packs and the Kibble Cup, a co-op raid stub and an Inu Verse export stub. Cosmetics only. Bones never cash out. Plug a real wallet in with `setWallet(adapter)`, using the same shape as `SimWallet`.
- **Tests:** `npm test` runs the headless rules, fee and ranking tests. `npm run e2e -- <url>` runs the Playwright checks; screenshots go to `proof/`.
